Two ideas sit underneath most of what I do.

The first is Spinoza's God : the notion that the universe isn't the handiwork of a distant creator but is itself the divine: a single, continuous substance where nature, math, and matter are all the same thing. It reframes studying the world as a way of paying close attention to something sacred.

The second is metanoia : a fundamental turning of the mind, the moment a way of seeing cracks open and rebuilds itself. College has been one long metanoia for me, and it's crazy that we are starting to see technology do the same thing with recursive self-improvement. It's not gonna be too long until Ultron makes an appearance.
