About

Two sides of the same person.

Half of me lives in equations, code, and research questions. The other half is outside with a camera. This page is both.

The academic side

I'm at Barrett, The Honors College at Arizona State, pursuing a B.S. in Math (Statistics) and a B.S. in Computer Science (Artificial Intelligence), with a minor in Studio Art — graduating May 2028.

I'm drawn to the places where math, data, and machine learning meet real systems: reinforcement learning and simulation for robots, computer vision, retrieval-augmented AI, and the unglamorous-but-crucial work of turning messy data into something trustworthy. I'm a Tillman Scholar and President's Scholar, and I've kept a 4.0 while chasing most of these rabbit holes.

The human side

Photography

My favorite thing to do. I started with wildlife — the patience of waiting for an animal to do something honest — and I've been moving into human photography, which asks for a different kind of patience.

Art

The Studio Art minor isn't an accident. I like making things with my hands and my eye, not just my keyboard — it feeds the way I see everything else.

Sports

Varsity basketball and soccer in high school. A knee injury slowed that down, so these days it's mostly intramural volleyball — still competitive, just easier on the joints.
