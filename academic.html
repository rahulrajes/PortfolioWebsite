notif Chrome extension · Developer Sep 2026

Zone out for thirty seconds in a big lecture and you miss the iClicker poll, and there goes your participation grade. I built notif to fix that. It sends a desktop notification and plays a sound the second a poll goes live on iClicker or TopHat. A MutationObserver watches the page for new polls, and I had to route the audio through an offscreen document to sneak past the site’s security policy. It doesn’t collect any of your data, and you can grab it on the Chrome Web Store.

Semantic-Layer Analytics Agent CSE 412 Databases · Team of 4 Fall 2026

My databases class project, built with three teammates. You type a question like “what were sales by seller state?” and get a real answer from a Brazilian e-commerce dataset with about 100k orders. If you hand a question straight to an LLM, you often get SQL that looks right and returns the wrong number. So we added a semantic layer: tables that spell out what each metric means and which columns it comes from. The agent (LangGraph and Gemini, with tools over MCP) figures out what you’re asking, looks up your terms, writes the SQL, and runs it read-only on Postgres. Watch the demo.
