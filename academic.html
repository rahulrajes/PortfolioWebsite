Rahul.AI Personal AI · A digital twin In design

Rahul.AI is a personal language model — a digital twin you can actually talk to. The idea is to fine-tune a base model on my own corpus (messages, emails, writing, and captured voice) and pair it with a retrieval layer: my data embedded into a vector store and pulled in at query time, so answers stay grounded in things I've actually said and done. With some persona conditioning on top, the goal is a model that responds the way I would rather than a generic assistant. It's still early — I'm mapping out data collection, privacy, and how much can be learned through fine-tuning versus retrieval.

Semantic-Layer Analytics Agent CSE 412 Databases · Team of 4 Fall 2026

A databases-course team project: an LLM-powered analytics assistant that lets a junior finance analyst ask revenue and reconciliation questions in plain English over a Brazilian e-commerce dataset (Olist, ~100k orders). Handing questions straight to a model tends to produce plausible-but-wrong SQL, so we put an explicit semantic layer in between — metadata tables that define every metric, dimension, and join once and map them to real columns. A LangGraph agent (Gemini Flash, tools served over an MCP server) classifies intent, resolves the user's terms against that catalog, compiles deterministic SQL, and runs it read-only on PostgreSQL — returning the rows plus a plain-English summary of exactly what was computed.
